Terms & Conditions & Privacy Policy

People & Process Excellence • Visteria Consulting Services Private Limited
Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Policy Version: v2.4-PPE
Operational Transparency Notice: The terms and policies described herein accurately reflect the actual technical architecture, database data flows, and administrative role boundaries of the People & Process Excellence platform. Specific enterprise service level agreements (SLAs), client retention schedules, and organizational policies are subject to confirmation by Visteria management and applicable client corporate agreements.

1. Purpose of the People & Process Excellence Survey

The People & Process Excellence platform is an enterprise assessment and organizational development solution provided by Visteria Consulting Services Private Limited. Its primary purpose is to collect structured employee feedback regarding workplace culture, operational workflows, leadership effectiveness, process efficiency, and organizational well-being. Collected data is transformed into actionable organizational insights that assist enterprise leaders in fostering continuous workplace improvements.

2. Employee Participation and Responsibilities

Participation in scheduled survey cycles is conducted on behalf of your employer in connection with organizational improvement initiatives. As a participant, you agree to:

  • Provide honest, constructive, and accurate feedback reflecting your authentic workplace experience.
  • Protect the confidentiality of your employee login credentials and survey access links.
  • Refrain from sharing login codes or submitting survey responses on behalf of other colleagues.
  • Adhere to your organization's professional code of conduct when providing optional open-ended text commentary.

3. Confidentiality and Handling of Employee Feedback

We treat all employee feedback with high confidentiality. Individual survey responses (Likert scale ratings and commentary) are aggregated at the department, branch, and company levels to produce generalized statistical summaries, sentiment trends, and organizational indexes. Reporting dashboards provided to enterprise management do not display raw scores mapped to employee names in executive summaries. Technical access to underlying database records is strictly restricted to authorized system administrators under role-based security controls.

4. Prohibited Misuse of the Platform

Users of this platform shall not:

  • Submit intentionally false, defamatory, harassing, or abusive content.
  • Attempt to probe, scan, bypass, or breach authentication mechanisms or security boundaries.
  • Reverse engineer, scrape, or extract source data or administrative reports without authorization.
  • Introduce malicious scripts, automated bots, or interference agents into the survey submission API.

5. User Consent and Acknowledgement

By registering an account and checking the agreement checkbox, you explicitly acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these Terms & Conditions and the associated Privacy Policy. You understand that your feedback will be processed to generate organizational excellence analytics for your enterprise.

Data Protection & Governance Notice: Visteria Consulting Services Private Limited processes employee information in accordance with applicable data protection principles, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and relevant international privacy standards.

1. Personal Information Collected and Intended Use

When you register and use the People & Process Excellence portal, the system collects and processes the following categories of data:

  • Identity Data: Full name, corporate email address, auto-generated employee code, and optional contact number.
  • Organizational Context: Company affiliation, branch, department, and assigned survey cycle identifiers.
  • Feedback Responses: Likert scale ratings (1–5) across survey categories, open qualitative feedback comments, and post-survey platform ratings.
  • Authentication & Technical Metadata: Secure salted password hashes (bcrypt), timestamp of registration, session tokens, and password reset tokens. Plaintext passwords are never stored.

This information is used strictly to verify employee eligibility, deliver assigned department-specific survey questionnaires, and synthesize organizational analytics.

2. Data Storage, Access Permissions, Retention, and Deletion

All application data is hosted on enterprise cloud infrastructure with database access restricted through authenticated connection pools and network isolation. Access permissions follow least-privilege principles:

  • Super Administrators: Authorized personnel managing company onboarding, survey question templates, and technical platform reliability.
  • Company Leadership: Authorized access limited strictly to their own enterprise’s aggregated reports, department analytics, and AI summaries. Cross-company access is strictly barred.
  • Retention Period: Survey records and associated analytics are maintained for the active duration of the evaluation cycle and administrative reporting period as designated by the client contract [Subject to client agreement and legal retention policies].
  • Account & Data Deletion: Requests for employee record erasure or department re-assignment may be submitted through designated corporate administrators or Visteria's Data Protection Officer.

3. Processing of Survey Responses and AI-Generated Insights

Survey responses are analyzed using structured statistical aggregations and intelligent natural language processing algorithms (including Eagle AI / Gemini API integrations). AI processing is used to:

  • Calculate category-level health scores, engagement distributions, and operational heatmaps.
  • Synthesize key organizational themes, strengths, and priority areas for leadership focus.
  • Identify systemic process friction and recommend constructive workplace training frameworks.

AI processing does not make automated individual decisions affecting employment or disciplinary action; insights are intended solely for organizational process improvement.

4. Limitations of Anonymity and Confidentiality

For absolute transparency: Although responses are aggregated to protect employee confidentiality, this system does not claim complete cryptographic anonymity. Each response record is associated with an employee ID and survey cycle in the database to verify participation, prevent duplicate voting, and support survey cycle tracking. Furthermore, if open-ended text answers contain self-identifying details or urgent disclosures of legal violations, health hazards, or severe workplace harassment, duty-of-care protocols may necessitate appropriate administrative review under enterprise compliance policies.

5. Applicable Privacy Rights and Legal Obligations

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have statutory rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to confirm whether your personal data is being processed.
  • The right to access and review your basic profile information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate profile attributes.
  • The right to register a grievance with the data fiduciary or relevant regulatory authority.
